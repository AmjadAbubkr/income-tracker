# Roadmap: IncomeTrack

IncomeTrack is a local-first business income and sales tracker. The v1
milestone is complete and the shipped architecture is intentionally offline,
with IndexedDB as the authoritative data store.

## Phase Details

### Phase 1: Local Authentication & Sign-in Shell

Three historical plans established authentication. The final offline
implementation is verified in `phases/01-backend-foundation/01-VERIFICATION.md`;
the original backend implementation is retained only as historical context.

### Phase 2: Product & Inventory Catalog

Three historical plans plus a retroactive final verification record cover local
product/category CRUD, custom categories, and optional stock tracking.

### Phase 3: Sales Recording

The final workflow supports batch checkout, sale history editing/deletion, date
and product filters, and atomic inventory reconciliation.

### Phase 4: Expense Log

The final workflow supports categorized expense entry, editing/deletion, and
date/category history filters.

### Phase 5: Subscriptions & Recurring Logic

Business and customer subscriptions generate recurring ledger entries through
atomic, idempotent catch-up processing.

### Phase 6: Visual Aesthetics & Dashboard

Dashboard and analytics share period calculations for summaries, charts, top
products, and recent activity.

### Phase 7: Notifications

Low-stock and near-term subscription notifications use the agreed thresholds,
refresh after mutations, and translated copy.

### Phase 8: Local Settings

Currency, theme, EN/FR/AR language, RTL, profile, and backup controls are
available through the local settings flow.

### Phase 9: Release Safety and Data Correctness

Integer minor-unit money, user isolation, atomic checkout/subscription/restore,
and regression coverage made the offline app release-safe.

### Phase 10: Complete Remaining v1 Workflows

Sales and expense history, reporting, notifications, profile email editing,
accessibility, and localization were completed and verified.
