<objective>
Complete the local sales and expense workflows with durable, user-scoped
history, individual edit/delete operations, and the agreed filters while
preserving exact money values and inventory correctness.
</objective>

<tasks>

<task type="auto" tdd="true">
<name>Task 1: Add ownership-safe individual financial mutations</name>
<files>src/utils/database.ts, src/utils/storage.ts, src/stores/incomeStore.ts, src/stores/expenseStore.ts, tests/financial-history.test.ts</files>
<action>
Add update methods for income and expenses through the database and storage
facades, then expose matching Zustand actions. Income update/delete must be
transactional with the products store: restore the prior sale quantity and
reserve the replacement quantity so tracked inventory cannot drift, including
when a sale changes product or quantity. Every read and mutation must retain the
current-user ownership checks introduced in Phase 9. Expense edits must update
only the owned record. Add regression tests for successful edits/deletes,
cross-user rejection, insufficient replacement stock, and inventory
reconciliation.
</action>
<verify>`npx.cmd vitest run tests/financial-history.test.ts` passes, including
the ownership and inventory cases; `npm run typecheck` passes.</verify>
<acceptance_criteria>
- Individual sales and expense records can be updated without bulk rewriting.
- Deleting or changing a sale leaves product inventory equal to the remaining
  recorded sales.
- A failed sale edit leaves both the old record and inventory unchanged.
- Cross-user IDs cannot be updated or deleted.
</acceptance_criteria>
</task>
